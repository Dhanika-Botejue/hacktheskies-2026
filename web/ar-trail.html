Camera trail test

Uses ARCore (camera + motion sensors) to track where you walk. Hold or strap the phone upright on your chest with the back camera facing forward. "Up" on the trail is the direction you face when tracking starts.

Android + Chrome only for now. Tracking works best with light and textured surroundings; blank walls, darkness and people filling the view make it lose track.
